## 🗺️ The Sizing & Risk Playbook: The Half-Kelly Rule

Traditional investors often make the mistake of over-allocating to hot, high-valuation, or pre-revenue hype cycles. To protect capital and mathematically guarantee long-term survival, we enforce a strict **Fractional Kelly / Margin of Safety** rule to our allocations:

*   **Long Odds Require Small Bets:** In high-risk, high-payout environments (like early-stage startups, where failure rates are > 90% but the payoff on exit can be 100x+), individual bet sizes must remain extremely small. We never allocate more than **1% to 2.5% of our total investable capital** to a single pre-revenue venture.
*   **The Humility Buffer:** Because human estimates of success are inherently over-optimistic, we always **cut our calculated allocation size in half (Half-Kelly).** This gives up only 25% of the peak theoretical compounding rate while slashing drawdowns by 50%. It guarantees that even if 95% of our individual bets fail, our core fund remains structurally unbreakable.

## 🔬 Due Diligence & Auditing Frameworks

*When auditing a potential company, we ignore marketing decks and TAM projections, focusing instead on three ruthless screens:*

1.  **The "Forensic" Ledger Audit:**
    *   Verify the actual, raw bank statements and ledger transactions of the last 12 months. Ensure there are no hidden cost-centers, unvouched cash burn, or complex subsidiary transaction networks.
2.  **The Counter-Positioning Screen:**
    *   Identify exactly what the startup is doing that the heavily funded, legacy competitors are *structurally paralyzed* from copying because doing so would damage their existing business models.
3.  **The "Default Alive" Timeline:**
    *   If expenses remain constant and growth continues on its current trajectory, does this company reach profitability before running out of cash? We do not back permanent cash-burn machines.
