## 📚 Summaries & Key Takeaways

### 1. The Psychology of Money
* **Author:** Morgan Housel
* **Summary:** An exploration of how behavior, ego, pride, and psychology influence financial decisions far more than mathematical spreadsheets. 
* **Key Takeaways:**
  * **Survival over Returns:** Staying financially unbreakable allows compounding to do its magic over decades.
  * **Freedom as Wealth:** The highest dividend money pays is the ability to control your time.

### 2. 7 Powers
* **Author:** Hamilton Helmer
* **Summary:** The definitive strategic framework for how businesses construct durable economic barriers (moats) to maintain persistent differential returns.
* **Key Takeaways:**
  * **The 7 Powers:** Scale Economies, Network Effects, Counter-Positioning, Switching Costs, Branding, Cornered Resource, and Process Power.
